## 🧭 About me

I care about building digital experiences that make a real difference: products that work well and add value to people's lives.

As **Team Lead Development at eHealth Ventures Group** I guide frontend, backend and QA developers working on digital health platforms such as Dokteronline, SeeMe-nopause and Breinfijn. I set the technical direction, improve quality and delivery, and work closely with product and business to turn ideas into well-executed solutions.

Technology is never the goal for me. It's the tool I use to create something meaningful and lasting, for users and for the teams I work with.
